Magnetic levitation control (Quanser MAGLEV)

Team of 3 · MATLAB, Simulink, QUARC
pole placementPI / PIVfeed-forwardreal-time hardware
+57.2 rad/sunstable open-loop pole
≈0.1 mmhold offset on the real rig
0steady-state error in simulation
  • Modelled the electromagnet–ball plant, linearised it at a 6 mm gap, and designed a nested controller by pole placement: a PI coil-current loop plus a PIV position loop with feed-forward.
  • In simulation the current loop met its 1.5% overshoot spec, and the loop rejected a 0.1 N sustained load (about 15% of the ball’s weight) with zero final error. Position overshoot was 10–11% against a 5% target; we traced it to a closed-loop zero and gains designed at 6 mm but used at 9 mm.
  • On the real rig (QUARC, Q8-USB, VoltPAQ-X1) the ball lifted off, was captured and held at about 7.9 mm for an 8 mm setpoint at roughly 1 A. Capture was oscillatory (about 4 s) and the larger 10 mm step wasn’t tracked, so gain scheduling is the next step.
  • Watch the levitation demo · try the game mode to feel the problem yourself.

Peltier vaccine carrier with PCM buffer

Team of 3 · Python simulation study
thermal modellingphase change materialsvalidationsensitivity analysis
108 minto reach 8 °C at 35 °C ambient
≈3.1 hholdover with 0.4 kg PCM (under 5 min without)
3–7%cooling-power error vs datasheet, 4.5–6 A
  • Simulation study (no prototype) of a portable carrier holding 2–8 °C: a single-node thermal model, a TEC1-12706 module model calibrated from its datasheet, thermostat control and a PCM enthalpy buffer.
  • At 35 °C it cycles in the 5–8 °C band at about 56 W (1.7 h on a 120 Wh pack). One module can’t hold the band at 40 °C and above.
  • Verified the code against exact solutions, validated against the manufacturer’s curves, and ran ±20% sensitivity on the estimated inputs. The electrical model could not be validated because the datasheet disagrees with itself, and the report says so.
  • Reviewed 15 sources on thermoelectric carriers and PCM. Code, figures and report
